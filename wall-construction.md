# Wall construction in the viewer

## What the model shows

The standard ground-floor exterior walls use separate insulation and masonry
volumes. Their cut faces show the distinction; room-facing surfaces retain
their paint and tile finishes.

The typical project section is **200 mm EPS insulation and 250 mm masonry**.
The geometry preserves the existing wall boundaries, room sizes,
window frames and door openings. It is a design visualization, not a survey of
the completed wall assembly.

The south atrium return W6 retains its existing 460 mm envelope. Its split is
200 mm EPS and a 260 mm nominal core region, not a claim that 260 mm bricks
were specified. This unreconciled coordinate difference is preserved rather
than moving the office wall to force a uniform 450 mm total.

## Dimensional basis

- Architectural technical report, section 4.14.2: EPS 70F, at least 200 mm.
- Assembly S01a and window/corner detail 403: 200 mm EPS with nominal 250 mm
  masonry. The revised loft plan retains the 200 mm insulation dimensions.
- A later masonry substitution specifies 240 mm bricks. That material change
  is recorded separately; it does not silently relocate the model's room or
  opening coordinates.
- The drawings exclude final surface finishes from their dimensions. Plaster,
  adhesive, reinforced coat and render are not compressed into the nominal
  450 mm wall envelope or represented as separately dimensioned layers.

## Scope limits

Narrow portal returns, blind-box heads, reinforced-concrete zones, gables,
plinths and garage walls are not detailed construction assemblies in this
preview. Do not infer their build-ups from the standard wall section.

The original 160 mm mineral-wool specification belongs to the timber-clad
gable assembly S01f, not a 160 mm EPS zone on the rendered main walls. Cancelled
timber cladding is not restored by this visualization.

Use the project drawings for the nominal geometry. Keep budget substitutions
and as-built measurements distinct; a contradictory quotation must not
silently override a dimensioned drawing.
