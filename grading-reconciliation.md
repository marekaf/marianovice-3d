The western platform is **7 × 15 m**, including the sauna area. Its eastern bank is 0.5 m wide, falling approximately 0.55–0.60 m into the green strip. The north/east boundary banks are approximately 2 m wide and meet the existing surveyed fence feet. Fence positions and heights are unchanged.

South of the western platform, a 0.5 m transition leads to a 0.5 m shallow strip at 397.48–397.45 m. These widths are scaled approximations from the drawing, not dimensioned setting-out values. The strip joins the retained garden terrain and preserves existing paths.

South of the apron, the soil falls from beneath the paving to a low line at 396.40 m west and 396.35 m east. The model uses a 0.5 m strip measured out from the retained apron edge; this depth is also a scaled approximation. The low line then joins the surveyed southern boundary through the garden bank.

Ground and finished surfaces remain separate. Grass levels describe ground; paving and floor marks describe exposed finished surfaces with bedding below. House-side gravel adds the existing modelled build-up above the rough ground. The dashed pergola/sauna levels are interpreted as their exposed surfaces; the rough-grading drawing does not specify their construction layers.

## Retained garden features

The drawing omits the greenhouse, raised beds, pond, firepit, bench and garden paths. They remain in the model. The greenhouse and bed court follow the 397.50 m western platform. The pond rim follows the 396.40 m lawn. The firepit keeps its independent 396.50 m surface. Paths connect the revised levels, and their rendered paving and walking heights use the same surface definitions.

The tank label is **8–10 m³**, as drawn. Its footprint and accessible cover remain in their established positions.

## Items requiring drawing or survey clarification

- The western spot marked **396.95 / −0.100** is inconsistent with the 397.00 house datum. The model uses **396.95**, equivalent to **−0.050**.
- The southwest high-bank spot marked **397.50 / +0.350** is also inconsistent: the relative label would mean **397.35**. The drawing's intended southward counterfall cannot be resolved from these conflicting labels. The northern counterfall segment uses the unambiguous **397.48 / 397.45** marks; its southward continuation remains a coordination item.
- The drawing specifies a **5.6%** entrance ramp. The retained apron and surveyed gate require approximately **6.1% average along the modelled centreline**. The gate has not been raised to force the nominal slope.
- The model retains its **4.00 m apron depth** and existing gate/driveway outline. The drawing labels approximately **4.03–4.06 m** at the apron; these small setting-out differences are not resolved by moving surveyed boundaries.
- Sauna and pergola positions are retained from the existing garden layout. The sauna-to-house gap is **2.691 m**, versus **2.25 m** drawn; the pergola's west face is **1.148 m** east of the house's northeast corner, versus **1.50 m** drawn. The pergola's south gap is **1.499 m**, matching the drawn **1.50 m**. Their revised levels do not resolve these separate setting-out differences.
- The drawing shows a **vehicle-rated drainage channel near the driveway gate and an outlet to an infiltration drain**. These remain unmodelled utility details; the existing sewer inspection chamber and rainwater tank do not represent this connection. Channel setting-out, invert levels and infiltration design are not established by this reconciliation.
- The garden paths are retained design proposals. Their revised slopes are measured by the regression checks; they are not an accessibility approval.

## Parking beside the driveway

The 4 m driveway is a through lane. A Scala and A6 together occupy 3.69 m before mirrors, leaving only 0.31 m for both edge clearances and the gap between cars. Parking one along this lane does not provide dependable access to or from the carport. A separate parking pocket and a vehicle swept-path check would be a further layout change.
