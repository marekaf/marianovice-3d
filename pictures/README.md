# Pictures

Photoreal pictures made from model views with `scripts/pictures`. Camera, sizes and positions follow the 3D model; where the model has no material, the picture shows one choice, listed per file. Items marked "not in the model" are ideas in the picture only.

## Garden

- `sauna-v1-house.jpg`: sauna building from the house side. 5 x 2.5 m block under one flat roof: closed sauna (hot room with the big window, changing room behind) and the open covered part with the tub. Chosen: horizontal chamfered larch cladding, natural honey tone, clear oil; larch deck; anthracite window frame and roof edge.
- `sauna-v1-shelter.jpg`: closer view of the covered part. Softub Legend, round soft-sided, 180 cm across, 61 cm high. Chosen: slate-grey vinyl sides, grey lid folded off, grey cube step beside it.
- `sauna-v1-evening.jpg`: the covered part and the sauna window at blue hour in autumn: lit hot room, downlights under the roof, steam over the tub, low path lights.
- `pergola-v1-timber-house.jpg`: pergola variant (a) timber, from the house side. Chosen: 15 x 15 cm glulam spruce posts on black steel bases, double beams, knee braces, slatted timber roof, warm mid-brown oak stain; climbing roses on posts and roof edge. Floor: light grey concrete slabs 60 x 60 cm.
- `pergola-v1-alu-house.jpg`: pergola variant (b) aluminium, from the house side. Chosen: matt anthracite powder-coated frame, 12 x 12 cm posts, 20 cm perimeter beam, no braces; fixed louvre blades running front to back. Climbers only at the front posts. Floor: warm grey porcelain tiles 60 x 120 cm.
- `pergola-v1-alu-close.jpg`: closer view of variant (b). The camera is closer than in the model view.
- `pergola-v1-alu-evening.jpg`, `pergola-v1-timber-evening.jpg`: the closer view at blue hour, both variants: linear pendant on, warm uplights at the posts, candles on the table.
- Both pergola variants keep the model's furniture: table for eight with a light ceramic top and oak base, eight mustard-yellow chairs, the slatted privacy screen along the fence, linear pendant light, two terracotta planters.
- `firepit-v1-house.jpg`: fire pit from the house side, the pergola at the left. Corten ring 1 m across and 27 cm high, round seating area 4 m across, three oak block benches 1.2 m x 42 cm x 45 cm in an arc open toward the house. Chosen: warm-beige hoggin with a slim steel edge, weathered silver-brown oak.
- `firepit-v1-close.jpg`: closer view, same choices.
- `firepit-v1-evening.jpg`: the close view at dusk with a wood fire.
- `atrium-v1-garden.jpg`: atrium from the garden: lawn, stepping stones, beds with grasses, climbers on wires, the deck with two lounge chairs, side table and planter. Chosen: warm grey-brown composite deck, black-frame lounge chairs with off-white cushions, pale concrete side table.
- `atrium-v1-deck.jpg`: from the atrium deck out to the garden, the raised beds at the right. The curved concrete shape in the middle is the path slab, drawn as an odd sculpted piece.
- `raisedbeds-v1-house.jpg`: the four raised beds (1 x 2 m, 60 cm high) on the 4.2 x 6.2 m gravel pad, the greenhouse and the sauna behind. Chosen: untreated larch boards, light grey fine gravel, drip lines, tomatoes on canes, lettuce, herbs, kale.
- `raisedbeds-v1-close.jpg`: closer view, same choices.
- `greenhouse-v1-beds.jpg`: the 2 x 2 m greenhouse from the raised beds, the sauna at the right. Gable roof, eaves 1.9 m, ridge 2.65 m, 40 cm dwarf wall, roof vent, 90 cm door. Chosen: light grey aluminium frame, clear glass, pale grey boarded dwarf wall, timber potting bench.
- `greenhouse-v1-close.jpg`: closer view at the door corner.
